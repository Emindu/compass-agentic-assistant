# Customer complaints handling policy

> MOCK DATA — fictional document created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Commitments

- Acknowledge every complaint within 1 business day.
- Resolve within 7 business days, or explain the delay and give a new date.
- Disputed card transactions: provisional credit within 10 business days when the claim is valid.

## Channels

Branches, contact centre (24/7), mobile app, email and the bank website.

## Escalation

Customers who are not satisfied may escalate to the Financial Ombudsman of Sri Lanka.

## Service outages

When a system outage affects customers, the contact centre uses the pre-approved outage script
and records each call under the incident number so compensation can be assessed.
