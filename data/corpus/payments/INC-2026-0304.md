# INC-2026-0304: Weekend CEFTS connectivity loss after client certificate lapsed

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

From 01:00 on Saturday until 07:30, the cefts-adapter could not connect to the national
instant payment switch. Outgoing instant transfers were queued and then sent as slower SLIPS
batch transfers.

## Impact

- Duration: 6 h 30 min, overnight on a weekend
- About 4,100 transfers fell back to next-day SLIPS settlement
- Low customer impact because of the hour, but regulatory reporting flagged the SLA breach

## Timeline

- 01:00 cefts-adapter logs `certificate_expired` from the switch side
- 01:05 On-call paged; adapter retries fail
- 06:15 Payments on-call confirms the switch rejects our client certificate
- 07:10 PKI team issues a replacement certificate
- 07:30 Connectivity restored

## Root cause

The client certificate the bank presents to the switch reached its expiry date. The renewal
action item from INC-2025-1207 (automated expiry monitoring) covered the card switch only; the
CEFTS certificate lives in a different keystore that the new monitor did not scan.

## Resolution

New certificate issued and loaded. Keystore inventory started for every external connection.

## Action items

- PAY-2340: Inventory all outbound client certificates across payment adapters
- PAY-2341: Extend expiry monitoring to every keystore found in the inventory
