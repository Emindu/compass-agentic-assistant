# INC-2026-0125: Salary-day payment delays from payments-ledger slowdown

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

On 25 January 2026 (salary day), outgoing fund transfers and bill payments queued for up to
40 minutes. The payments-ledger service stopped accepting new postings for long periods.

## Impact

- Duration: 1 h 50 min of degraded service (11:05–12:55)
- About 96,000 transfers delayed; none lost
- Corporate payroll uploads timed out for 14 business customers

## Timeline

- 11:05 p99 latency on payments-ledger rises from 120 ms to 28 s
- 11:20 Thread dumps show request threads waiting on `HikariPool-1 - Connection is not available`
- 11:45 A finance reconciliation report found running long queries against the primary database
- 12:10 Report killed; connection pool recovers gradually
- 12:55 Queues drained

## Root cause

The payments-ledger database connection pool (max 50 connections) was exhausted. A month-end
reconciliation query, scheduled during business hours, held connections for minutes while
salary-day traffic was 3.4x normal. New requests waited for a connection until they timed out.

## Resolution

Reconciliation job stopped. Pool size temporarily raised to 80 after DBA review.

## Action items

- PAY-2290: Move reconciliation jobs to the read replica and outside 09:00–17:00
- PAY-2291: Alert when pool utilisation stays above 85% for 5 minutes
- PLT-1120: Capacity test payments-ledger at 4x salary-day load
