# INC-2026-0712: Biller aggregator timeouts cause bill payment outage

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

On 12 July 2026, utility bill payments failed for 48 minutes. The external biller aggregator
slowed down, and our own clients overwhelmed it while it recovered.

## Impact

- Duration: 48 min (10:20–11:08)
- About 9,800 failed bill payments; 140 customers charged twice, reversed next day

## Timeline

- 10:20 Aggregator response time rises from 300 ms to 9 s
- 10:24 biller-gateway thread pool saturated; mobile app shows "try again" and users tap repeatedly
- 10:35 Aggregator reports our traffic at 5x normal and blocks our IP range
- 10:55 Retry policy hot-patched to back off; block lifted at 11:05
- 11:08 Success rate recovered

## Root cause

A thundering-herd retry pattern, the same failure mode as INC-2026-0214 on a different
integration. biller-gateway used a fixed 500 ms retry with no jitter and no retry cap, and
the mobile app retried on top of it. PAY-2305 (backoff with jitter) had only been applied to
the CEFTS switch client, not to biller-gateway.

## Resolution

Retry policy changed to exponential backoff with jitter; idempotency check added on payment
reference.

## Action items

- PAY-2460: Shared resilient HTTP client library for all external payment integrations
- PAY-2461: Disable client-side automatic retries in the mobile app for payment submissions
