# INC-2026-0214: Instant transfer outage amplified by gateway retries

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

On 14 February 2026, instant interbank transfers (CEFTS) failed for 72 minutes. A short latency
spike at the national switch turned into a full outage on our side.

## Impact

- Duration: 72 min (18:03–19:15)
- About 23,500 failed or duplicated-then-reversed transfers
- 312 duplicate debits, all reversed within 24 hours

## Timeline

- 18:03 National switch latency rises to 6 s for about 4 minutes
- 18:05 payments-gateway request rate to the switch jumps from 220 to 900 requests/s
- 18:09 Switch starts rejecting our connections (rate limit)
- 18:40 Gateway retries disabled by feature flag; traffic returns to normal levels
- 19:15 Switch lifts the block after a call with their operations desk

## Root cause

payments-gateway retried timed-out switch calls immediately, up to 3 times, with no backoff,
no jitter and no circuit breaker. The mobile app also retried on its own. Together this
amplified traffic about 4x and the switch throttled the bank, turning a 4-minute upstream
latency spike into a 72-minute outage. Retries without idempotency keys also caused duplicate
debits.

## Resolution

Retries disabled via feature flag; duplicate debits reversed by the operations team.

## Action items

- PAY-2305: Exponential backoff with jitter and a retry budget for all switch calls
- PAY-2306: Circuit breaker on the switch client
- PAY-2307: Idempotency keys on transfer requests
