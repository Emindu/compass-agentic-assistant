# INC-2025-1207: Card authorisations declined after card switch handshake failures

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

Between 08:12 and 10:47 on 7 December 2025, about 38% of debit and credit card authorisations
were declined with response code 91 (issuer unavailable). The card-switch-connector could not
establish TLS sessions with the international card switch.

## Impact

- Duration: 2 h 35 min
- About 41,200 declined POS and e-commerce transactions
- 1,870 customer calls to the contact centre; social media complaints peaked at 10:00

## Timeline

- 08:12 Error rate alarm on card-switch-connector (handshake_failure on outbound TLS)
- 08:40 Network team rules out firewall changes
- 09:55 Engineer notices the client certificate presented to the switch expired at 00:00 UTC
- 10:30 Renewed certificate deployed from the PKI team
- 10:47 Authorisation success rate back to 99.6%

## Root cause

The mutual-TLS client certificate used by card-switch-connector to authenticate to the card
switch expired. Renewal was tracked in a personal calendar of an engineer who had left the
team; there was no automated expiry monitoring for outbound client certificates.

## Resolution

Certificate renewed and deployed manually. Connector pods restarted to load the new keystore.

## Action items

- PAY-2231: Add certificate expiry monitoring with 30/14/7-day alerts (owner: Payments Platform)
- PAY-2232: Move certificate renewals into the PKI team's automated renewal pipeline
- PAY-2233: Update runbook RB-PAY-003 with the keystore reload procedure
