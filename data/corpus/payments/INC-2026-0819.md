# INC-2026-0819: Card switch TLS failures after CA chain change

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

On 19 August 2026, 22% of card authorisations failed for 41 minutes after the card switch
rotated its server certificate to a new intermediate certificate authority.

## Impact

- Duration: 41 min (14:02–14:43)
- About 12,600 declined authorisations

## Timeline

- 14:02 handshake_failure errors on card-switch-connector, only on some pods
- 14:15 Failing pods found to run an older image with an outdated truststore
- 14:35 Truststore updated with the new intermediate CA and pods rolled
- 14:43 Recovered

## Root cause

Certificate lifecycle management again. The card switch announced the intermediate CA change
six weeks earlier by email, but the notice went to a shared mailbox nobody monitored. Our
truststore did not contain the new intermediate certificate, so TLS validation failed. The
expiry monitor from PAY-2231 checks our own certificates only, not trust chains of partners.

## Resolution

Truststore updated across all connector images; image build now pulls the CA bundle from PKI.

## Action items

- PAY-2511: Subscribe the payments on-call rotation to partner certificate notices
- PAY-2512: Contract test that validates partner TLS chains daily in staging
