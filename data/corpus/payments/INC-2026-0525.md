# INC-2026-0525: Holiday peak bill payment failures

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

During the Vesak holiday shopping peak on 25 May 2026, bill payments and QR merchant payments
failed intermittently for 65 minutes with HTTP 503 from payments-ledger.

## Impact

- Duration: 65 min (19:40–20:45)
- About 18,300 failed payments; customers were asked to retry
- Merchant acquiring complaints from two large supermarket chains

## Timeline

- 19:40 503 rate on payments-ledger exceeds 10%
- 19:52 Database CPU normal, but application threads blocked waiting for DB connections
- 20:15 Payments-ledger scaled from 6 to 10 pods; errors increase
- 20:30 Scaled back to 6 pods; pool settings reviewed
- 20:45 Error rate returns to baseline as traffic falls

## Root cause

Requests could not obtain a database connection: all connections in each pod's pool were in
use. Scaling out pods made it worse, because every new pod opened its own pool and the
database hit its global max_connections limit. The pool-utilisation alert from PAY-2291 was
configured but routed to a muted channel.

## Resolution

Traffic subsided naturally. Pod count and pool size aligned to the database limit.

## Action items

- PLT-1188: Introduce PgBouncer between payments-ledger and the database
- PAY-2402: Route pool-utilisation alerts to the payments on-call rotation
