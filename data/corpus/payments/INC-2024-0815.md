# INC-2024-0815: SWIFT gateway connection failure from expired certificate

> MOCK DATA — fictional incident created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Summary

On 15 August 2024, outward SWIFT messages were held for 3 hours because the SWIFT gateway's
TLS certificate had expired.

## Impact

- 640 outward remittances delayed to the next settlement window

## Root cause

An expired certificate on the swift-gateway host. The renewal reminder was sent to a former
employee's mailbox.

## Action items

- PAY-1802: Shared mailbox for certificate renewal notices
