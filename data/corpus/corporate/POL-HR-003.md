# Hybrid work policy

> MOCK DATA — fictional document created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Eligibility

Head office staff in eligible roles may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager
approval. Branch and operations roles are on-site.

## Working remotely

- Use only bank-issued laptops connected through the corporate VPN.
- Do not work on confidential or restricted data in public places.
- Be reachable during core hours, 10:00 to 15:00.

## On-call staff

Engineers on an on-call rotation may work remotely during their on-call week regardless of
the 2-day limit, provided they can reach a laptop within 15 minutes.
