# Acceptable use of generative AI assistants

> MOCK DATA — fictional document created for the Compass assessment POC.

## Approved tools

Only the bank's internal assistant (Compass) may be used with internal or confidential data.
Public AI tools may be used for public information only.

## Rules

- Do not paste customer personal data or account numbers into any AI tool.
- Verify AI answers against the cited source before acting on them.
- AI assistants must not give customers financial or investment advice on the bank's behalf.
- Report answers that look wrong or unsafe using the feedback button.

## Accountability

The employee who acts on an AI-generated answer remains accountable for the decision.
